## 💡 5. 빠른 생각 & 메모 (Inbox)
* **Office 클라우드 자동 업데이트(`ignoregpo=1`) 완전 잠금 & 해제 PowerShell 원라이너** (상세 가이드: [[30_사내_M365_계정꼬임_및_DRM충돌_롤백_가이드]]):
  * **잠금**: `Start-Process powershell -Verb RunAs -ArgumentList "-NoProfile -Command Set-ItemProperty -Path 'HKLM:\SOFTWARE\Policies\Microsoft\cloud\office\16.0\Common\officeupdate' -Name ignoregpo -Value 0 -Type DWord -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue; Set-ItemProperty -Path 'HKLM:\SOFTWARE\Policies\Microsoft\cloud\office\16.0\Common\officeupdate' -Name enableautomaticupdates -Value 0 -Type DWord -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue; New-Item -Path 'HKLM:\SOFTWARE\Policies\Microsoft\office\16.0\common\officeupdate' -Force | Out-Null; Set-ItemProperty -Path 'HKLM:\SOFTWARE\Policies\Microsoft\office\16.0\common\officeupdate' -Name EnableAutomaticUpdates -Value 0 -Type DWord; Disable-ScheduledTask -TaskPath '\Microsoft\Office\' -TaskName 'Office Automatic Updates 2.0' -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue; Disable-ScheduledTask -TaskPath '\Microsoft\Office\' -TaskName 'Office Serviceability Manager' -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue"`
  * **해제**: `Start-Process powershell -Verb RunAs -ArgumentList "-NoProfile -Command Set-ItemProperty -Path 'HKLM:\SOFTWARE\Policies\Microsoft\cloud\office\16.0\Common\officeupdate' -Name ignoregpo -Value 1 -Type DWord -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue; Set-ItemProperty -Path 'HKLM:\SOFTWARE\Policies\Microsoft\cloud\office\16.0\Common\officeupdate' -Name enableautomaticupdates -Value 1 -Type DWord -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue; Remove-ItemProperty -Path 'HKLM:\SOFTWARE\Policies\Microsoft\office\16.0\common\officeupdate' -Name EnableAutomaticUpdates -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue; Enable-ScheduledTask -TaskPath '\Microsoft\Office\' -TaskName 'Office Automatic Updates 2.0' -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue; Enable-ScheduledTask -TaskPath '\Microsoft\Office\' -TaskName 'Office Serviceability Manager' -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue"`
